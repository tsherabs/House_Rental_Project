# Illustrative demo imagery

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool for this project, then resized
to 1440px and compressed to WebP. These depict fictional homes, not real listings.
The demo seed labels the listings and explains that these images are shared.

- `thimphu-home.webp`: “Photorealistic editorial architectural photograph of a
  welcoming modest three-storey Bhutanese residential apartment building in a
  green Thimphu valley, cream plaster, traditional dark timber window frames and
  subtly painted lintels, balconies, pine-covered foothills, soft morning light,
  plants beside a stone path. No people, text, logos or watermarks. Fictional demo
  housing. Building slightly right of centre for flexible cropping.”
- `valley-living-room.webp`: “Photorealistic editorial real estate photograph of
  a modest modern rental living room in Thimphu, oak floor, cream walls, timber
  window frames with subtle Bhutanese carpentry, sage sofa, woven rug, wooden
  table, plants and books, large window overlooking forested mountains and valley
  buildings. Natural morning light, realistic proportions, no people/text/logos.
  Fictional illustrative demo property.”

Generated assets remain local and require no third-party image hosting at runtime.
